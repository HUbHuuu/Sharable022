Metal-enclosed Switchgear

We possess advanced sheet-metal and copper-busbar production lines, equipped with state-of-the-art devices such as laser cutting machines, bending machines, turret punches, welding robots, and integrated copper busbar punching, shearing, and bending machines. We specialize in OEM production of various power equipment for customers.

Our product range extends from low-voltage GCK and GGD low-voltage power distribution boxes to medium-voltage primary center-mounted cabinets, ring-main units, and even ZGS-12/0.4 packaged substations (American style), as well as frequency conversion cabinets, explosion-proof control cabinets, shore power boxes, etc.

Especially in the energy storage field, we have remarkable capabilities. We can provide OEM production of air-cooled energy storage systems, integrated energy storage units, step-up converter integrated units, liquid-cooled energy storage systems, etc.

We not only offer complete design solutions but also have a reliable assembly and production management system and strictly implement comprehensive factory-out quality tests to ensure that every product is of high quality, providing you with all-around and high-quality solutions for your power needs!
